About Me

Hi, I'm Prince Mark Barcelon, a 19-year-old Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at Cebu Institute of Technology – University. I'm currently living in Talisay City, Cebu, and this portfolio reflects both my progress and my interests as I continue learning in the field of technology.

Before pursuing IT, I originally planned to take Civil Engineering. I've always had a strong interest in mathematics, along with an appreciation for structure and design. I enjoyed understanding how things are built and paying attention to the details behind them.

However, I decided to shift into Information Technology despite having no prior background in programming or technical skills. Starting from zero was challenging, especially in an environment where many already had experience, but it also pushed me to adapt and learn more intentionally.

As I continue studying, I've begun to see the connection between my past interests and my current field. The logical thinking I developed through math helps me approach programming problems, while my eye for structure supports how I organize and design my work.

Outside of academics, I enjoy listening to music and playing sports such as volleyball. These help me maintain balance while working toward improving my skills in IT.

I may still be in the early stages of my journey, but I am committed to continuous learning and growth in this field.
